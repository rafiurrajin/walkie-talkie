# LAN Walkie-Talkie

A tiny walkie-talkie that runs in your web browser. Everyone on the same Wi-Fi or network can talk to everyone else, no apps, no accounts, no fuss.

## How it works for you

1. Open the page on your phone or laptop.
2. Type your name and hit **Join**.
3. Press **TALK** when the line is free, and speak. Everyone else hears you live.
4. Press **STOP** when you're done, so someone else can have a turn.

## Good to know

- **One speaker at a time.** If someone else is talking, you'll see their name and the TALK button stays greyed out. There's no queue, so just wait until the line is free.
- **You won't hear yourself.** Only the others do.
- **Your microphone is only on while you're talking.** It turns off as soon as you press STOP or leave.
- **Names are just labels.** Two people can pick the same name, and nobody will stop you.
- **If you get disconnected,** refresh the page and join again.
- **Works on Chrome and Firefox,** on devices connected to the same network as the server.

## What it's not

No chat history, no recordings, no multiple channels, and no fancy profiles. It's a simple digital walkie-talkie for a small group of people you trust.
